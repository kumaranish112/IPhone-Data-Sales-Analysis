# IPHONE DATA SALES ANALYSIS PROJECT
The iPhone Data Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing Flipkart data to understand customer preferences and sales trends for iPhones in India. It identifies the top 10 highest-rated iPhones, the number of ratings and reviews they have received, and highlights the iPhone with the most reviews. The project also explores the relationship between sale price and the number of ratings, as well as the impact of discount percentage on customer engagement. Additionally, it determines the least and most expensive iPhones in the market, along with their full specifications. The analysis provides valuable insights for pricing, marketing, and inventory strategies.

## FEATURES OF THIS PROJECTS:-
#### 1. WHAT IS THE TOP 10 HIGHEST-RATED IPHONES ON FLIPCART IN INDIA?
#### 2. HOW MANY RATINGS DO THE HIGHEST-RATED IPHONES ON FLIPKART HAVE?
#### 3. WHICH IPHONE HAS THE HIGHEST NUMBER OF REVIEWS ON FLIPKART?
#### 4. WHAT IS THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE SALE PRICE OF IPHONES AND THE NUMBER OF RATINGS ON FLIPKART?
#### 5. WHAT IS THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE DISCOUNT PRECENTAGE AND THE NUMBER OF RATINGS OF IPHONES ON FLIPKART?
#### 6. CAN YOU FIGURE OUT THE LEAST EXPENSIVE AND MOST EXPENSIVE IPHONES IN THE INDIAN MARKET, ALONG WITH ALL THEIR SPECIFICATIONS?
